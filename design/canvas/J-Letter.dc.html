Tien Shan Water Account Statement no. 1, record to 2025

To: the dispatcher, Naryn hydropower cascade
Subject: snow depth above the reservoir, 1981 to 2025

Snow depth in the upper Naryn basin has risen by 0.20 cm per decade, or 0.88 cm over the 45 years on record. The long-term average is 12.82 cm.

The change is statistically proven (Mann-Kendall test, p = 0.048), so it is unlikely to be chance.

For your decision. Take this change into account when you plan the winter drawdown.

For comparison. In the Vakhsh basin of the Pamir the same measure fell by 1.94 cm per decade over the same years (p = 0.003).

Computed from NASA POWER model data, one grid cell of about 50 km. A record of the past, not a forecast. The model contains no glaciers.
