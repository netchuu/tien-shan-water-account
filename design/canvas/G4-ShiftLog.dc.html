What the record says

Naryn, Tien Shan

  • Snow depth is rising by 0.20 cm per decade. Proven.
  • Snowfall is rising by 0.062 mm per day per decade. Proven.
  • Rain and snow together are rising by 0.166 mm per day per decade. Proven.
  • Air temperature shows no proven change (+0.07 °C per decade, p = 0.338).

Vakhsh, Pamir

  • Snow depth is falling by 1.94 cm per decade. Proven.
  • Snowfall is falling by 0.081 mm per day per decade. Proven.
  • Rain and snow together are falling by 0.079 mm per day per decade. Proven.
  • Air temperature is rising by 0.52 °C per decade. Proven.

Read it as

The two cascades are on different paths. Plan the Naryn drawdown with more snow in mind, and the Vakhsh drawdown with less.

Record of 1981 to 2025 from a NASA model. Past trend, not a forecast.
